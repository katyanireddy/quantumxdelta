Rules & Regulations

Creating A Safe & Positive Environment

These guidelines help maintain discipline, respect, and academic excellence throughout the school.

📚 Attendance Policy

  • • Minimum 75% attendance required.
  • • Leave applications must be submitted.
  • • Long absences require approval.

👕 Uniform Guidelines

  • • Proper uniform mandatory.
  • • Sports uniform on activity days.
  • • ID card must be worn.

🎓 Discipline

  • • Respect teachers and students.
  • • No bullying or misconduct.
  • • Maintain classroom decorum.

🚌 Transport Rules

  • • Follow bus supervisor instructions.
  • • Maintain discipline in vehicles.
  • • Report issues immediately.

Parent Responsibilities

  • ✓ Ensure regular attendance.
  • ✓ Monitor academic progress.
  • ✓ Maintain communication with teachers.
  • ✓ Inform school about emergencies.
  • ✓ Encourage positive behaviour.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if attendance falls below 75%?

Students may not be eligible for examinations.

Is mobile phone usage allowed?

No, unless specifically permitted by school authorities.

Can parents meet teachers anytime?

Meetings are scheduled during designated parent-teacher interactions.
